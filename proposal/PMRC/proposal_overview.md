# URJA-2047 India — PMRC Proposal Overview

**Title:** URJA-2047 India: A Geospatial Energy Transition Intelligence Framework for Energy Demand, Energy-Service Equity, Renewable Feasibility and Climate-Resilient Pathways towards Viksit Bharat 2047

**Primary thematic area:** Energy, Sustainability and Climate Change

## National challenge

India must simultaneously reduce the economic and strategic burden associated with petroleum and natural-gas imports, decarbonise rapidly growing energy demand, and ensure that development in underserved regions does not reproduce fossil-fuel lock-in. A population-scale transition requires evidence that distinguishes present energy use from adequate energy-service need and then connects that need to technically feasible, affordable and climate-resilient renewable pathways.

## Core logic

**USE → NEED → OPPORTUNITY → PATHWAY**

Cross-cutting optimisation dimensions: **COST | CARBON | CLIMATE | EQUITY**.

The programme integrates a national 1-km geospatial data cube, PESM, ESAG, ETFE, a 2047 demand model, scenario analysis, Pareto optimisation, SEUPA mapping, pilot validation and the URJA-2047 Digital Twin.

## Preliminary proof of concept

A national 1-km structural CatBoost PESM model was trained on 35,000 observations and evaluated using 100-km spatially grouped cross-validation. The operational structural model achieved OOF R² = 0.6165 and RMSE = 1.1037. The final national raster contains 2,280,332 valid analytical cells; 15.83% have standardized PESM ≥ 1 and 7.67% have PESM ≥ 2. PESM is a screening signal, not a direct measure of electricity access or energy poverty.

Preliminary national solar, wind and solar-wind complementarity products have also been generated to demonstrate the feasibility of the opportunity side of the framework.

## Five-year programme

The Chair will expand the proof of concept into six linked objectives: national data cube; energy use and adequacy; renewable feasibility; 2047 demand-resource-grid-storage matching; equitable techno-economic pathways; and validation/digital-twin/policy translation.
